About CMMC Day

CMMC Day is a one-day educational event designed for defense contractors, IT professionals, program managers, and business leaders who need to understand the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) framework and what it means for their organization.

📋

Practical Guidance

Leave with actionable steps you can take immediately to advance your CMMC readiness.

👥

Expert Speakers

Hear from certified practitioners, assessors, and industry leaders on the front lines of CMMC compliance.

🔗

Network & Connect

Meet peers, partners, and vendors navigating the same challenges in the defense industrial base.

🎯

All Levels Welcome

Sessions are designed for both those new to CMMC and those already deep into their assessment journey.
